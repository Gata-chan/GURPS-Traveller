> The FarStar Association is a loose organization of independent trade brokers rather than a real corporation, but its service is still valuable. If an independent merchant captain is willing to commit to a regular route, FarStar will see to it that he always has the best cargo available when he arrives. On the other hand, woe betides the captain who is consistently late, or who misses a pickup. Getting on the FarStar blacklist can be very bad for business . . .
