> Redwing Express is a recent addition to the ranks of [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Terran Confederation\|Terran]] space industries, established in 2158. It may be a new company, but it has a reputation as a hard worker, and has expanded very rapidly. It specializes in private courier service to every world in the [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Terran Confederation]], carrying data, small cargoes, or important passengers. Redwing’s distinctive paint jobs, guaranteed service, and “can do” attitude take the customer’s mind off its high fees.
