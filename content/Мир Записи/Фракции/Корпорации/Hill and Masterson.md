> Hill and Masterson (often called “H&M”) is a large [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Страны/United States of America\|American]] manufacturing concern, with installations on [[Мир Записи/Места/Планеты/Terra]] and several of the colony worlds. The firm specializes in heavy vehicles, and is the primary supplier of [[gravitic vehicles]] under contract for the [[Terran Confederation Army\|Confederation Army]]. It also produces one of the most popular civilian grav vehicles, the Hill and Masterson “Phoenix Air/Raft.”
