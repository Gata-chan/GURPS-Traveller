> This firm operates no starships of its own, but it builds ships in the [[Мир Записи/Места/Планеты/Terra\|Terran]] shipyards. It is an important supplier of parts and systems for other major shipbuilders in the [[Мир Записи/Места/Планеты/Terra\|Terran]] system. It is also a major military contractor. Finally, it is an important financial backer for a variety of deepspace ventures. Named after its most famous CEO, [[Yukio Hasegawa]], Hasegawa Limited has gone through a number of name changes and other reorganizations since its foundation in 2092. Most recently (2168), the firm’s headquarters were moved to London, and it was reincorporated under British law to take advantage of favorable [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Страны/European Union\|European]] regulations concerning deep-space industry. The current CEO is [[Yoshira Ehara]], the grandson of [[Yukio Hasegawa]]. Although Hasegawa established the [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Free Traders Foundation]], he always kept his firm separate so that the independence of the Foundation would not be compromised; that tradition continues to the present day.
