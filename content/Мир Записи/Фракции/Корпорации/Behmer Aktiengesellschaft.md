> Behmer AG (nicknamed “The Bag”) is a consulting firm founded in the early 21st century, working with small [[Мир Записи/Места/Планеты/Terra\|Terran]] nation-states to solve local economic and social problems. During the 2050s the company became a major contractor to the [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Страны/European Union]]’s generation-ship program. Behmer personnel left [[Мир Записи/Места/Планеты/Terra]] as some of the earliest interstellar colonists, prepared to set up new governments when the generation ships reached their destinations. The first colony to be established, at [[Мир Записи/Места/Планеты/Prometheus]], began with a Behmer staffed administration that was quite successful. Today, Behmer AG has become [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Terran Confederation]]’s leading provider of administrative services for low-population colony worlds. Industrial firms that establish off-world colonies often call on Behmer to help set up and maintain local government. The [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Terran Confederation]] has also contracted with Behmer for civilian administration on occupied [[Vilani]] worlds; the company is given some credit for the successful integration of [[Nusku]] into the [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Terran Confederation]].
