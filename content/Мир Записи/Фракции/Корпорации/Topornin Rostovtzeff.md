> Topornin/Rostovtzeff T.O.O. (Tovarichestvo c Ogrannichnuu Otvetstvenuu or “limited liability partnership”) is the largest [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Страны/Russia\|Russian]] company engaged in offworld trade. Within the [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Terran Confederation]] itself it is a relatively minor player, but it is investing heavily in potential trade into the [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Vilani Imperium\|The Imperium]]. It has been quite successful in opening markets on the [[Kimashargur\|kimashargur]] worlds closest to [[Мир Записи/Места/Планеты/Terra]], sometimes using innovative techniques (i.e. smuggling) to avoid [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Vilani Imperium\|The Imperium]] restrictions on [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Terran Confederation\|Terran]] trade. Topornin/Rostovtzeff is the second largest participant in the [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Free Traders Foundation]] (after the [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Корпорации/High Frontier Development Consortium]], and operates several exploratory-trade ships of its own.
