> Founded in 2123, the Free Traders Foundation was a nonprofit organization intended to promote open markets and interstellar trade. The brainchild of [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Страны/Japan\|Japanese]] industrial magnate [[Yukio Hasegawa]], the Foundation helped [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Terran Confederation\|Terran]] entrepreneurs acquire ships and begin carrying [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Terran Confederation\|Terran]] goods into [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Vilani Imperium\|The Imperium]], at first without the knowledge or approval of the [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Terran Confederation\|Terran]] government or [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Vilani Imperium\|The Imperium]] administrators. Although it occasionally continues to act at cross-purposes with the [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Terran Confederation\|Terran]] government, the Foundation is now a very powerful organization. It is particularly influential in the new [[Nusku]] Republic, where its member corporations have invested heavily. Naturally, the Foundation is even less popular with [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Vilani Imperium\|The Imperium]] officials than with the [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Terran Confederation\|Confederation]] government. Many observers fear that Free Traders’ activities within [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Vilani Imperium\|The Imperium]] may be provoking a new outbreak of war.
