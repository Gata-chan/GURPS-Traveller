> India is the most populous nation on the planet, a lively democracy and a major center of [[Мир Записи/Места/Планеты/Terra\|Terran]] culture. Although it is technologically backward, it can provide a great deal of manpower for projects all over the [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Terran Confederation]]. In particular, it is one of the largest sources of recruits for the [[Terran Confederation Army\|Terran Army]].
