> Technically, the European Union is an alliance of nation-states, but after almost 200 years of cooperation the alliance is so close as to almost constitute unification. The three leading nations of the Union remain France, Germany, and the United Kingdom. The Union stresses “soft power” in world affairs, concentrating on economic and moral influence rather than direct political or military control. The Union is the second largest economic power on [[Мир Записи/Места/Планеты/Terra]], trailing the [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Страны/United States of America\|United States]] by the smallest of margins. It has a very extensive space program, and is the largest single contributor of ships for the [[Terran Confederation Navy\|Terran Navy]].
