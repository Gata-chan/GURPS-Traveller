> After the breakup of the old Soviet Union, many of its smaller republics eventually joined the [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Страны/European Union]]. Russia’s nationalistic tendencies led it to chart an independent course. Its vast natural resources and enormous land area have made it a power to be reckoned with on [[Мир Записи/Места/Планеты/Terra]], although development has been stubbornly slow. Although Russia is not a first-tier nation, it had a space program of its own before [[Vilani]] contact, and so its presence in space is still larger than its rank on [[Мир Записи/Места/Планеты/Terra]] would indicate.
