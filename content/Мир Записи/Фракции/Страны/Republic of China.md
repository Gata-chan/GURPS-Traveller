> In the course of the 21st century, the Communist government of China gradually faded into a more moderate state, still authoritarian but with trappings of Western democracy and capitalism. China has a very large population, and has done a great deal to catch up with the most advanced nations over the last century. As a result it has a powerful voice in [[Мир Записи/Фракции/Межзвёздные Государства/Terran Confederation\|Terran]] politics. China encourages its population to migrate to the colonies, by subsidizing transportation costs for families willing to relocate. China also financed and equipped one of the early Phoenix Expeditions.
